## Third-party game and mod definitions

This project does not copy or redistribute third-party definitions, code, or assets.
Its conditional XML patches target definitions supplied at load time by RimWorld and
optional installed mods. Those definitions remain the property of Ludeon Studios and
their respective authors.

The patch set names or targets definitions from [XND] Nocturnal Animals, Dogs Mate,
Some Like It Rotten, Zoology, Animals Forage (Continued), Vanilla Expanded Framework,
and other animal mods. Their inclusion as patch targets does not make them dependencies
unless declared as such in `Mod/About/About.xml`.
